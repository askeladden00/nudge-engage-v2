# NPS Feedback Findings — Engage v2

Source: 10 raw NPS free-text responses. Analysis below is based only on these 10 responses — not a statistically representative sample, but directionally consistent with the interview synthesis (see [research/interview-synthesis.md](interview-synthesis.md)) and the existing Engage v2 hypothesis (see [strategy.md](../strategy.md)).

## Summary

Every one of the 10 responses raised a complaint; none were purely positive. The two largest themes — nothing new happens after week 1, and no reason to come back — are two sides of the same problem and directly reinforce the current retention hypothesis: users get an initial "aha," then the app gives them nothing further to act on.

## Themes Mentioned More Than Once (ranked by frequency)

| Rank | Theme | Frequency | Responses |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 (tie) | Nothing new happens after the first week — content is static/repetitive | 3 | #2, #7, #8 |
| 1 (tie) | No compelling reason to return — low salience, users forget the app exists | 3 | #1, #5, #10 |
| 3 (tie) | Users want proactive, directive guidance ("tell me what to do"), not passive tracking | 2 | #3, #6 |
| 3 (tie) | Nudges/content feel generic or impersonal | 2 | #4, #9 |

Note: #6 (goal never mentioned again) sits at the intersection of "nothing new" and "wants proactive guidance" — it's both a staleness complaint and a coaching complaint. Grouped here under proactive guidance since the specific frustration is the app failing to follow up on a stated goal.

## Praise vs. Complaints

**Praise (all partial — each is paired with a complaint in the same response):**
- #2: "The first week was genuinely eye-opening."
- #5: "Love the concept."
- #10: "The weekly email is the only thing keeping me engaged" (praise for the email specifically, not the app).

**Pure complaints (no praise attached):**
- #1, #3, #4, #6, #7, #8, #9

No response in this batch was unambiguously positive — worth flagging to Marcus on its own.

## Top 3 Actionable Issues

1. **Savings goals go unacknowledged after being set.** (#6) A user explicitly set a goal and says the app "never once mentioned it since." This is a narrow, concrete fix: surface goal progress somewhere the user will see it.
2. **The home feed/content doesn't change over time.** (#2, #7, #8) Three separate responses independently describe the same problem: nothing new to see after the first session. This is the most frequently cited issue and the most direct match to the existing Engage v2 hypothesis.
3. **Nudges feel random enough that users disable them entirely.** (#4) One user describes a specific irrelevant notification (a $12 coffee purchase) as the reason they turned off notifications altogether — turning off notifications forecloses the primary re-engagement channel the team has.
